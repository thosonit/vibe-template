Layout

A 4px base unit drives every gap. Use only the spacing scale. Padding is space inside a container; gap is space between siblings, and gap is preferred over margins. Related items sit closer than unrelated ones.

Control sizes

Heights are taller on touch screens and step down from 768px. Buttons and inputs placed side by side share one size. Change the size, never the padding, to give a control more room.

Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from shadow and tone, not borders. Cards and fields have no border, so a light shadow and the step in tone between background and surface separate them from the page. In the dark theme the level 1 shadow is dropped and the tone step carries the edge; level 2 becomes a 1px inner highlight.

Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Use primary for one main action per screen.
  • Pair every background token with a text color that meets 4.5:1.
  • Use semantic variants (danger, not "red button").
  • Keep radii and spacing on the defined scales.
  • Copy the HeroUI v3 component (structure, variants, sizes, radius, motion, states) when adding a new one, and record it in DESIGN.md.

Don't

  • Add borders to cards or fields. Use shadow and tone.
  • Hardcode colors or invent values. Use the tokens, so a new primary reaches every component.
  • Use the primary for status. The primary means brand and action, green means success.
  • Remove focus rings or animate layout properties such as width, height or margin.
