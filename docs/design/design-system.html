Design System — Alpha

Restraint printed in one bold color.

Verdant Editorial pairs an editorial layout — generous whitespace, a serif display face, near-black ink on warm off-white paper — with a single, unapologetically bold green accent, spent only where it matters.

01 — Color Palette

Quiet paper, one loud accent

Ink steps down in three tiers so hierarchy comes from contrast, not new hues. primary is reserved for actions and brand moments — it should never cover a large surface, only buttons, links, and small accents.

03 — Button Variants

Pill-shaped, confidently tappable

Buttons use rounded.full to read as confident actions — echoing the pill CTAs common in music/media brand UI. button-primary is the only component allowed to use the accent as a full-strength fill.

06 — Cards & Containers

Moderate radius, never a pill

Containers use a softer rounded.lg so they read as content surfaces, not controls — the opposite personality from the fully-rounded buttons above.

A

Editorial by default

Generous whitespace and a serif display face give every layout a printed, considered feel.

B

One accent, spent well

Green appears only on actions and brand moments — never as a background wash.

C

Two shape personalities

Pills for actions, moderate corners for containers — the two are never mixed.

09 — Elevation & Depth

A whisper of lift, never gloss

DESIGN.md doesn't define explicit shadow tokens, so depth is inferred here from the existing ink/surface tiers: shadows are tinted with ink at low opacity rather than pure black, keeping lift understated and print-like rather than glossy.

Flat
Resting on paper — page background, no shadow.
Raised
Default card level — subtle lift, ink @ 4–6%.
Floating
Menus, modals — used sparingly, ink @ 6–12%.
10 — Responsive Behavior

Two breakpoints, generous rhythm preserved

The editorial whitespace scale compresses one step at each breakpoint rather than disappearing — rhythm should stay legible even on mobile.
